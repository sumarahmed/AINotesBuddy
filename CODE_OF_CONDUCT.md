# Code of conduct

NotesBuddy contributors and maintainers are expected to create a respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and never share another person's meeting data.
- Use synthetic examples when discussing recordings or transcripts.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sharing confidential audio, transcripts, credentials, or customer data
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or infrastructure

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and
focused on the safety and health of the project community.
